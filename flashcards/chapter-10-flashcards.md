## Chapter 10: Full NCA-AMAD Practice Exam and 60-Minute Strategy — Flashcards

| # | Front (Question) | Back (Answer) |
|---|-----------------|---------------|
| 1 | NCA-AMAD exam: total questions and time limit? | Approximately 50-60 questions in 60 minutes. Exam tip: budget ~50-55 sec/question on first pass to leave an 8-10 min review buffer. |
| 2 | Target first-pass pace per question (assuming 55 questions, 60 min)? | ~50-55 seconds per question. This consumes ~48-50 minutes, reserving 10-12 minutes for flagged-question review. |
| 3 | What is the flag-and-skip rule for answering uncertain questions? | Always select a best-guess answer FIRST, then flag for review — never leave a flagged question blank. Protects your score if time runs out. |
| 4 | Recommended max percentage of questions to flag during a 60-min exam? | ~15-20% (roughly 8-12 of 55 questions). Flagging more than this leaves insufficient buffer time for review. |
| 5 | Scenario deconstruction: what do you do BEFORE reading the answer options? | Extract explicit constraints (latency, cost, scale, data sensitivity, agent count, etc.) and form your own expected answer. Prevents anchoring to distractor phrasing. |
| 6 | Domain-weighted review priority — how to allocate remaining review time? | Proportional to domain exam weight: review flagged questions from the highest-weighted domains (e.g., Agentic AI design, Data Processing) first, since fixing those has greater expected score impact. |
| 7 | Most common distractor pattern across all 8 NCA-AMAD domains? | An option that satisfies SOME but not ALL explicitly stated scenario constraints — partially-correct answers are the #1 trap, not deprecated or unfamiliar terms. |
| 8 | Skip-without-answering vs. guess-then-flag — which scores better under time pressure? | Guess-then-flag always wins: a blank answer guarantees 0 points, while a best-guess answer has nonzero expected credit even if never revisited. |
| 9 | When stuck between two plausible answers with no time to flag-and-return, what's the fastest tie-breaker? | Eliminate whichever option violates an explicit scenario constraint, then commit to the remaining option immediately. |
| 10 | Final-week review checklist — top priority activity? | Re-attempt previously missed practice questions and study the 'why wrong' explanations for incorrect options — targets your specific distractor blind spots instead of relearning everything. |
| 11 | Pacing checkpoint rule at the halfway time mark (30 min)? | You should have answered roughly half the questions (~27-28 of 55). If behind, tighten pace on new questions rather than resolving flags mid-exam. |
| 12 | First-pass review vs. full re-read under 2 minutes remaining — which strategy wins? | Constraint-based elimination on the current two candidate answers beats a full scenario re-read; re-reading risks losing time needed for remaining unseen or flagged questions. |
