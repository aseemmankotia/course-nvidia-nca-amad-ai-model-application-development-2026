# Chapter 5 Lab: Customization Spectrum: Prompting, RAG, PEFT, and Alignment

**Estimated time:** 20 minutes

## Exercise

Given three scenarios — add company facts to answers, permanently change the model's tone/style, and teach new domain vocabulary with limited GPU budget — choose RAG, instruction/PEFT fine-tuning, or full fine-tuning for each and justify the cost/accuracy tradeoff

## Key takeaway

Pick the cheapest technique that meets the requirement: prompting first, then RAG, then PEFT, then full fine-tuning only when necessary.
