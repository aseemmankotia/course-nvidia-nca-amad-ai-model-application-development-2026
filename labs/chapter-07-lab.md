# Chapter 7 Lab: The NVIDIA AI Stack: AI Enterprise, NIM, NeMo, and Blueprints

**Estimated time:** 20 minutes

## Exercise

Take the agentic support-chatbot design from Chapter 2 and annotate which NVIDIA component (NeMo, NIM, Blueprint, AI Enterprise) handles each piece: fine-tuning, serving, orchestration template, and enterprise support/security

## Key takeaway

NeMo builds and customizes models, NIM serves them, Blueprints give you a reference architecture to start from, and AI Enterprise is the supported platform underneath all of it.
