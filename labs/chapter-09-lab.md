# Chapter 9 Lab: Physical AI: Robotics, Simulation, and Digital Twins

**Estimated time:** 15 minutes

## Exercise

Sketch a simple physical-AI pipeline for a warehouse robot: simulate navigation in a virtual twin environment, train/validate the perception model, then deploy to the physical robot — label which stage reduces real-world risk and cost

## Key takeaway

Physical AI applies perception-and-action AI to the real world, with simulation and digital twins reducing cost and risk before physical deployment.
