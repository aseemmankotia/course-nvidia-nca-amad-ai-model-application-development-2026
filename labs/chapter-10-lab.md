# Chapter 10 Lab: Full NCA-AMAD Practice Exam and 60-Minute Strategy

**Estimated time:** 60 minutes

## Exercise

Complete a full timed 55-question practice exam simulation covering all eight domains in 60 minutes, then review a domain-by-domain score breakdown to identify weak areas for final review

## Key takeaway

Pace yourself at roughly one minute per question, flag-and-skip hard ones, and weight your final review time toward Agentic AI and Data Processing — the two highest-weighted domains.
